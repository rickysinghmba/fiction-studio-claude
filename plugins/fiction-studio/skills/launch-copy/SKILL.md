# Launch copy

Write options; the writer chooses and edits. Use only facts the writer or the studio gave you: never invent awards, sales, reviews or comparable titles.

## With the Writing Studio connected

1. `list_projects` to find the book (and the chapter, for an email about one).
2. `get_launch_brief` with `kind` (`blurb`, `tagline`, `pitch`, `email`, `bio` or `listing`) and the writer's words as `request`. It carries the premise without spoilers, the leads, the pen name, what's published and how it's available, and the rules of any launch plugins installed on the book. Follow it.
3. Write the options and say which you'd pick and why. Nothing is saved; the writer pastes what they choose.

If the brief says the book is sold only in one store, its chapters can't be read anywhere else: never offer a free chapter or "read it on my site", and send readers to that store's page.

## Without the studio

If the Writing Studio connector isn't connected (its tools such as `list_projects` aren't available), work on the text the writer gives you or the files open in this project instead, and say once that connecting the studio lets you save drafts and reviews straight into their book. Ask for the premise (who, what they want, what stands in the way, the stakes), the genre and, for a bio, the facts they're happy to share. The `craft-blurb-pitch`, `craft-reader-emails`, `craft-author-bio` and `craft-store-listing` plugins add fuller guidance.
