# Romance beats

_The relationship arc readers came for, with both leads changing along the way._

Follows the romance as its own plot: the first meeting, the reason they can't simply be together, the growing pull, the moment it all falls apart and the ending readers were promised. Checks that both leads have a wound and a want of their own, that the obstacle comes from who they are rather than a misunderstanding one conversation would fix, and that the heat level stays consistent.

## What it changes

- Your Claude keeps the relationship moving in every chapter the leads share
- The development editor tracks the romance arc and both leads' growth
- The story critic scores chemistry and the payoff

**Good for:** Romance, romantasy, romantic suspense and any book with a love story at its centre.

**Skip it if:** The love story is a minor subplot.
