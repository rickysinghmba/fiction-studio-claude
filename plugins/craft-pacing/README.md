# Pacing & structure

_Tension across the book: no long flat stretches, no endless peaks, a middle that pulls._

Has the development editor and the story critic map tension scene by scene, check that the midpoint changes the story's direction, that the darkest moment grows from the characters' own choices, and that the middle of the book doesn't sag. Openings and chapter endings get their own checks.

## What it changes

- The development editor scores tension and checks the big turns
- The story critic checks openings and chapter endings

**Good for:** Full manuscripts and second drafts.

**Skip it if:** You're still discovery-drafting and don't want structure notes yet.
