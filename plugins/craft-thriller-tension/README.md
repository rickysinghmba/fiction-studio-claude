# Thriller tension

_A clock that keeps ticking and stakes that rise every chapter._

Keeps the pressure on: a clear deadline, stakes that escalate rather than repeat, a hero who acts instead of waiting, an antagonist who is competent and moving, and twists that hold up on a second read. Flags chapters where the danger resets or nothing has got worse.

## What it changes

- Your Claude ends scenes with things worse than they began
- The development editor tracks the clock and escalation chapter by chapter
- The story critic checks whether twists survive a second read

**Good for:** Thrillers, suspense, action and spy fiction.

**Skip it if:** You write slow-burn literary suspense where quiet dread is the point.
